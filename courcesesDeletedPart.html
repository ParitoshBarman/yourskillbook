Your Skill Book

SKILL BOOK SKILL DEVELOPMENT CENTRE specializes in delivering high-quality vocational training to students, focusing on theoretical and practical aspects. Founded by experienced professionals Mr. Ashish and Ms. Pooja in 2021, SKILL BOOK has rapidly expanded from online classes during the Covid-19 pandemic to comprehensive offline programs. We offer a wide range of courses, including Computer Courses (NIELIT Accredited) and CADD Software Training, Mechanical Design, Civil and Architecture Design, Interior Design, Electrical/Plumbing Design, and more. Our curriculum is enriched with real-world projects, workshops, and seminars in collaboration with industry and educational institutions, ensuring our students are well-prepared for the challenges of today's technical fields.

With vast experience in training, we are uniquely positioned to understand and meet the precise skill set requirements of industries. Our training programs are designed to blend theoretical knowledge with practical, hands-on experience, effectively bridging the gap between industry demands and academic learning.

At SKILL BOOK SKILL DEVELOPMENT CENTRE, we are committed to ensuring that our industrial training translates into meaningful career opportunities for our students. We aim to provide skilled manpower that meets industry standards. Through strategic partnerships with leading companies, we facilitate placements in roles such as project design, computer application, and design operations across various sectors.

We uphold the highest standards of quality and compliance, aligning with NSDC standards to empower individuals with the skills necessary for success in engineering and technology.

Join us at SKILL BOOK SKILL DEVELOPMENT CENTRE and take the first step towards a rewarding career in the dynamic world of technical innovation and industry excellence.
